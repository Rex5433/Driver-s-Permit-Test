School bus

Red lights + stop arm = stop.

On a roadway divided by a barrier or unimproved median, opposite-direction traffic is not required to stop; traffic moving in the same direction still must.

Disabled vehicle

Move over when you can.

For a disabled stationary vehicle with flashing hazard lights, change lanes away. If you cannot, reduce speed to 10 mph below the posted limit and proceed cautiously.

Railroad

Never enter without room to clear.

Do not begin crossing tracks unless you can fully clear them without stopping. Never drive around a lowered gate.
